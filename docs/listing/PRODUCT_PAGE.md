## Free page copy

**Tagline:** Catch stray pixels, doubled corners and accidental transparency before you ship. Free, private, in your browser.

**Description:**

> Pixel Proofreader checks your PNG sprites for small technical slips that are easy to miss at 1×:
>
> - **Orphan pixels:** single pixels with no neighbour of the same colour (a slipped brush, leftover colour).
> - **Doubled corners:** extra pixels that make 1-pixel lines look thick at a bend.
> - **Partial transparency:** pixels that are neither fully opaque nor fully transparent, which can show up as halos in engines.
>
> Drop a PNG on the page. Issues appear as a coloured overlay on a crisp, zoomable view. **Your artwork is never changed**, and you can toggle any check you don't need.
>
> **Private by design:** your image is decoded and analysed entirely in your browser. No uploads, accounts, cookies, analytics or AI services. The page blocks all network requests.
>
> These are *suggestions* about technical pixel patterns, not judgements of your art. Break the rules on purpose whenever you like.
>
> Works offline: download the ZIP and open `index.html`.

## FAQ
- **Does it upload my images?** No. Everything happens in your browser or on your PC, and the page blocks network requests.
- **Will it change my files?** No. It only reads them. Pro exports are written as new files.
- **Is this "AI judging my art"?** No. It's a set of transparent, deterministic rules, each explained in the app. They are suggestions that you can turn off.
- **Which formats?** PNG only, in every PNG colour type (indexed, grayscale, RGB, RGBA, 1–16 bit, interlaced).
- **Size limit?** 4096×4096 pixels per image.
- **Refunds?** Owner to define, following itch.io's refund process.
- **Support?** Owner to define a contact, such as the itch.io community board.
