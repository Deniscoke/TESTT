Pixel Proofreader Free
======================

Open index.html in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).
Drop a PNG onto the page, or click "Choose a PNG".

- Everything runs locally in your browser. Your images are never uploaded.
- Checks: orphan pixels, doubled corners, partial transparency.
- Findings are suggestions about technical pixel patterns, not judgements
  of artistic quality. Toggle any check you do not need.

Free to use. See the Pixel Proofreader page on itch.io for the Pro edition.
